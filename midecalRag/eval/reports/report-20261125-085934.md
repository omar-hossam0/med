## Findings and recommendations

- Not-found correctness is 40% at the evaluated floor: irrelevant parents reach the LLM for absent facts. The prompt's rule 1 is the only guard unless a gate is enabled.
- No single dense-cosine floor separates relevant from irrelevant parents without dropping expected documents; the relevant/irrelevant distributions overlap. RELEVANCE_FLOOR stays 0.0.
