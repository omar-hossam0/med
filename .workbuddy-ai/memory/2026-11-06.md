## Doctor hero — second pass: full consultation scene (asset swap)

The user rejected the portrait crop and demanded the full doctor+patient scene
from the approved screenshot.

**Root cause of why the approved look was unreachable with the supplied asset:**
`Modern Digital Healthcare Consultation.png` / `doctor-hero-clinic.jpg` is a
marketing composite with three UI cards baked in at x 380–690 and 1185–1600.
The patient sits at x 0–356 and the doctor at 697–1146 — the "Today's Schedule"
card occupies the *only* gap between them. Geometry check: for a 232px-tall
banner, a card-free crop (495px wide) can only be ~200px wide before the head
overflows, so any crop that shows both people also shows a fake schedule card
directly above the real schedule panel.

**Decision:** use the other approved Medora consultation photo
(`Warm Doctor-Patient Consultation at Medora Healthcare.png`), cropped to
`doctor-hero-scene.jpg` (x 150–1672). It is card-free, so the composition can
match the approved target: full scene, doctor's head intact, patient in frame,
clinic + Medora wall logo visible.

**Layout:** photo panel 62% right, mint scrim 68% left with a 5-stop gradient,
copy max-w 47%, hero md:min-h 236, `object-[50%_3%]`. Mobile: 190px band.

**Known deviation:** the clean asset has the doctor on the LEFT and the patient
on the RIGHT (the reference has the patient left, doctor right) — mirroring would
reverse the Medora wall logo, so it was not mirrored.

`doctor-hero-portrait.jpg` and `doctor-hero-clinic.jpg` are kept in assets so the
previous composition can be restored in one line if the clean plate arrives.

## Doctor hero — organic transition (removed the hard vertical seam)

User: "the hero looks like two rectangles side by side, hard vertical boundary".

Cause: the photo panel was a rectangle whose left edge was covered by a scrim
that had already faded to ~40% opacity at that point, so the panel's edge showed
as a straight line.

Fix: the photo panel (w-58%, starts at 42%) now gets a **mask-image** that
dissolves its left edge (`transparent 0% → 0.5 @3% → 0.9 @6.5% → #000 @10%`), so
the section's mint gradient shows through — no seam, no scrim needed. Added one
broad translucent mint arc + edge wash (SVG, preserveAspectRatio=none) for depth.
`object-[26%_50%]` keeps the crop on the right so the patient survives at 1080.

Also scaled the hero/cards up to the design's real metrics (measured against
`Medora Healthcare Doctor Dashboard.png` at its native 1672 width, not at 1080 —
the mockup is a 1672 design, so comparing at 1080 makes my build look bigger):
- hero min-h 200/220/232, heading 23→31px by breakpoint, desc 12/12.5, CTA h-38
- stat cards: icon 40px, number 21px, py-3.5
- schedule rows: py-3, avatar 34, name 11.5, meta 9.5, time column 56px
- card titles 12.5px

Verified at 1672 (design width) and 1080 (user's width) — no overflow, no
truncation beyond one long patient name.
